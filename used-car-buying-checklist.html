Used-Car Buying Checklist in India

What to verify before paying an advance or signing transfer papers.

A careful inspection helps you identify obvious risks, but it cannot guarantee a vehicle is accident-free or mechanically sound. Arrange an independent inspection by a trusted mechanic and verify records through the relevant official channels before purchase.

1. Verify documents and ownership

  • Match the seller's identity with the registered owner's details and confirm authority to sell.
  • Check the Registration Certificate (RC), registration number, chassis and engine numbers.
  • Verify insurance validity, policy details and claim history where available.
  • Check PUC certificate, road tax status and pending challans through official portals.
  • Confirm whether a loan hypothecation is recorded and obtain the lender's required closure documents and NOC before transfer.
  • Ask for service invoices, warranty documents, spare keys and owner manuals.
  • Use the official transport department process for ownership transfer; do not rely only on a signed informal note.

2. Inspect the car in daylight

  • Look for uneven panel gaps, mismatched paint, overspray, rust and signs of poor repairs.
  • Check windscreen markings, lamps, mirrors, windows, door locks and all seat belts.
  • Inspect tyres for uneven wear, cracks and matching size; check the spare wheel and tools.
  • Look under the bonnet for leaks, damaged wiring, corrosion and missing covers.
  • Check the boot floor and cabin for dampness, unusual smells or water ingress.
  • Confirm that warning lights illuminate at ignition and go out as expected after starting.

3. Take a proper test drive

With the owner's permission and valid insurance, drive on a safe route that includes slow traffic and a smoother road. Listen for unusual engine, suspension or brake noises. Check steering alignment, clutch feel, gear changes, braking, air-conditioning, temperature behaviour and dashboard warnings. Do not test at unsafe speeds.

4. Estimate the full ownership cost

Do not compare only the asking price. Consider immediate repairs, tyres, battery, insurance renewal, scheduled service, fuel or charging, parking, tolls and any transfer-related charges. Ask for written estimates for known repairs before negotiating.

5. Before paying

  • Get an independent mechanic's written inspection report.
  • Check the vehicle's service and accident history as far as records allow.
  • Write down the agreed price, included accessories, known defects and delivery date.
  • Use traceable payment methods and obtain a signed receipt.
  • Complete the official ownership transfer and insurance update promptly.

Warning signs: pressure to pay immediately, refusal of an independent inspection, mismatched identification numbers, incomplete ownership documents, unexplained loan records or a seller who is not authorised to sell. Pause the transaction until these issues are resolved.

Where to verify

Use the Parivahan and relevant state transport department services for official vehicle and transfer information. Confirm insurance details with the insurer. A used-car inspection is not a substitute for official verification.
