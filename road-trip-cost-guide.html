How to Calculate Road Trip Cost in India

Estimate fuel, tolls, total travel expense and cost per person before you start your journey.

1. Calculate fuel required

Start with the total distance and the vehicle's mileage. If the trip is a return journey, use the complete round-trip distance.

Fuel required (litres) = Distance (km) ÷ Mileage (km/L)

2. Calculate fuel cost

Fuel cost = Fuel required × Fuel price per litre

3. Add toll charges

Add the expected toll amount to the fuel expense. This gives a simple estimate of the main road-trip costs.

Total trip cost = Fuel cost + Tolls

4. Split the cost

Cost per person = Total trip cost ÷ Number of travellers

Example

Suppose a car travels 300 km one way, returns to the starting point, gives 15 km/L and fuel is ₹105/L. The round trip is 600 km. Fuel needed is 600 ÷ 15 = 40 litres, so fuel cost is 40 × ₹105 = ₹4,200. If tolls are ₹600, the estimated trip cost is ₹4,800. With four travellers, that is ₹1,200 per person.
