What the text counter can help with

Use the counter to check a draft against a word limit, estimate reading time, or compare revisions. Word counts can differ slightly between apps because hyphenated terms, contractions, numbers and punctuation are not treated identically by every editor.

Reading-time estimate

Reading time is only an estimate based on an assumed average reading speed. Technical, legal or unfamiliar material usually takes longer than casual text; speaking time also depends on pauses and delivery.

For privacy, avoid pasting passwords, financial account details, private messages or other sensitive information into any online tool unless you are comfortable with how that tool handles data.

Word count and reading-time estimates

Word count is useful for school assignments, articles, reports, applications and social posts with length limits. Different platforms can count hyphenated words, contractions, symbols and languages differently, so treat this tool as a practical estimate if you must meet a strict publisher or submission rule. Reading time is approximate because reading speed varies with topic complexity, language, familiarity and accessibility needs.

Check text before submitting

For an assignment or application, follow the organisation’s own rules for what is included in the count—such as headings, references, footnotes or captions. For a speech, reading time depends on pauses and delivery pace. If your text contains private or confidential material, avoid pasting it into tools unless you understand where and how it is processed.

Frequently asked questions

Why does another website show a different word count?

Tools may treat punctuation, hyphenated terms, contractions and non-English text differently.

Is reading time exact?

No. It is a rough estimate based on an assumed reading speed, not a prediction of your actual pace.

Does word count include headings?

It counts the text supplied to the tool; the rules of your school, employer or publisher may differ.

Should I paste confidential text?

Only if you understand the tool’s data handling and are comfortable with it. Check the privacy policy for details.
