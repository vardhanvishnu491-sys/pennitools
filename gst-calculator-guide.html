GST Calculator Guide

Calculate GST amounts and understand GST-inclusive and GST-exclusive prices.

Calculate GST on a base price

GST amount = Base price × GST rate ÷ 100

For a base price of ₹1,000 and a GST rate of 18%, the GST amount is ₹180 and the GST-inclusive price is ₹1,180.

Find the GST-inclusive price

Inclusive price = Base price + GST amount

Find the base price from an inclusive amount

When the displayed price already includes GST, the base amount must be derived using the applicable GST rate rather than simply subtracting the rate percentage from the total.

Base price = Inclusive price × 100 ÷ (100 + GST rate)
