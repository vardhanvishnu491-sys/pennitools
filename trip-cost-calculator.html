Free Trip Cost Calculator for India

Use this road trip cost calculator to estimate the money needed for a car or other road journey. Enter distance, vehicle mileage, current fuel price, toll charges and number of travellers. The calculator instantly shows fuel required, total trip cost and cost per person.

Calculate fuel and petrol cost for a trip

Fuel required is calculated from distance and mileage, then multiplied by the fuel price you enter. This makes the tool useful as a petrol cost calculator, diesel trip calculator, fuel cost calculator or travel cost calculator for trips in India.

Include tolls and calculate a round trip

Add your expected toll charges to get a more realistic travel budget. Select Round trip when you will return to the starting point; the calculator doubles the distance and fuel requirement automatically.

Understand total travel cost and cost per kilometre

Your total trip cost combines fuel expense and tolls. Divide the total by distance to understand your effective travel cost per kilometre, or use the displayed cost per person to compare the budget for a shared road trip.

Example: planning an India road trip

If a trip is 300 km one way, the vehicle gives 15 km/L and fuel costs ₹105/L, selecting round trip calculates 600 km and about 40 litres of fuel. Add toll charges and the number of travellers to estimate the complete shared trip budget.
