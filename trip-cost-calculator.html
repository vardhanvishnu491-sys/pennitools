How the Trip Cost Calculator Works

This free trip cost calculator helps you estimate the cost of travelling by car. Enter your distance, vehicle mileage, fuel price, toll charges and number of travellers to calculate the total trip expense and cost per person.

Calculate fuel cost for a trip

The calculator estimates fuel required from your distance and mileage. Fuel cost is then calculated using the fuel price you enter. This makes it useful as a petrol cost calculator, diesel trip calculator or general fuel cost calculator for road trips in India.

Include toll charges and round trips

Add your expected toll charges to get a more complete road trip cost estimate. Select the round-trip option when you are returning to the starting point so the distance and fuel requirement are doubled automatically.

Compare travel cost per kilometre or person

The results show total distance, fuel needed, total trip cost and cost per person. Use these figures to plan a family trip, compare routes or estimate the travel budget before you leave.
