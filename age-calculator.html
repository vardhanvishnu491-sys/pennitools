How age is calculated

Age in completed years increases on the birthday, not simply after dividing the number of days lived by 365.25. The calculator compares the selected birth date with today's date and reports completed years, remaining months and days.

Example

If someone was born on 15 January 2000 and today is 26 October 2026, their completed age is 26 years, 9 months and 11 days. The result can differ by one day depending on the current date and local time zone.

Important notes

  • Enter the date from the correct calendar and double-check day/month order.
  • For legal eligibility, school admission, employment, benefits or official forms, follow the rules and reference date specified by the relevant authority.
  • This is a convenience calculation, not an official proof-of-age document.

Frequently asked questions

Is age calculated using 365 days per year?

No. This calculator compares calendar years, months and days. Leap years and the lengths of calendar months mean that dividing total days by 365 can produce a different result.

Why can my age differ by one day on another device?

The result depends on the current date used by the device and its local time zone. Check the date and the date of birth if a result is close to a deadline.

Can I use this result for official eligibility?

Use the exact reference date and age-counting rules specified by the relevant school, employer, government agency or other authority. This tool is not proof of age.

Does the calculator predict a future age?

It is intended to calculate age as of the current date. For official future eligibility or application deadlines, verify the specified cutoff date independently.
