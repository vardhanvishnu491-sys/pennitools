EMI Calculator Guide

Understand monthly loan EMI, interest and total repayment before comparing a loan.

What is EMI?

Equated Monthly Instalment (EMI) is the regular payment used to repay a loan over a chosen tenure. The payment depends mainly on principal, interest rate and loan duration.

Standard EMI formula

EMI = P × r × (1+r)n ÷ ((1+r)n − 1)

Here, P is the loan principal, r is the monthly interest rate expressed as a decimal, and n is the number of monthly payments.

Example

For a ₹5,00,000 loan at 10% annual interest over 5 years, convert the annual rate to a monthly rate and use 60 monthly payments. The PenniTools calculator can perform the calculation instantly and show the repayment estimate.

How tenure changes the result

A longer tenure can reduce the monthly EMI but may increase the total interest paid. A shorter tenure generally increases the monthly payment while reducing the number of interest-bearing months.
