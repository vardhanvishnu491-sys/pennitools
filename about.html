About PenniTools

PenniTools is a free, practical toolbox designed to make everyday calculations and document tasks simple, fast and accessible.

Our goal is to give students, workers, homeowners, carpenters, construction professionals, vehicle owners, small businesses and everyday users useful tools without unnecessary complexity.

About the Founder

Mosali Vishnuvardan Reddy is the founder and creator of PenniTools. He develops, maintains and continuously improves the website with the goal of providing useful, simple and accessible online calculators and browser-based tools.

For questions, feedback, calculation corrections or technical issues, please contact: vardhan.vishnu491@gmail.com

What you can find

  • Construction, area, length and carpenter calculators
  • Tiles, paint, concrete and material estimators
  • Finance, EMI, salary and savings calculators
  • Vehicle, fuel, EV and trip-cost tools
  • Image and PDF utilities
  • Audio transcription and other productivity tools
  • Everyday, date, percentage and education calculators

Our approach

We aim for clear inputs, understandable results, mobile-friendly design and browser-first processing where practical. Calculators are intended to provide useful estimates; important financial, construction, legal or professional decisions should always be verified with the relevant official or qualified source.

Continuous improvement

PenniTools is being actively improved. If you find a calculation error, confusing result or broken tool, please report it so it can be investigated and corrected.
