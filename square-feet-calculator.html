How to calculate square feet

When measurements are in inches:

Length × Width ÷ 144 = Square Feet

Example:
77 × 33 = 2,541 square inches
2,541 ÷ 144 = 17.65 sq ft

When measurements are in feet:

Length × Width = Square Feet
