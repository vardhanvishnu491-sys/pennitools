Square Feet Calculator

Calculate area from inches or feet for carpentry, flooring and construction.

Area

How to calculate square feet

Measurements in inches: Length × Width ÷ 144 = square feet.

Measurements in feet: Length × Width = square feet.

For example, a 10 ft × 12 ft room has an area of 120 sq ft. If measurements are 77 inches × 33 inches, divide the product by 144 to convert the result to square feet.

Where a square feet calculator is useful

Use this calculator for room measurements, flooring estimates, ceiling areas, walls, furniture panels, plywood work, carpentry and basic construction planning. Keeping the measurement unit consistent helps avoid calculation errors.

Square feet vs square inches

One square foot contains 144 square inches. That is why measurements given in inches use the ÷ 144 formula. When both dimensions are already in feet, simply multiply length by width.

Worked examples you can check

Room measured in feet: 10 ft × 12 ft = 120 sq ft. This is the floor area before allowing for cupboards, cut-outs or material wastage.

Small panel measured in inches: 77 in × 33 in = 2,541 sq in. Divide 2,541 by 144 to get about 17.65 sq ft.

Metric measurements: a 3 m × 4 m room has an area of 12 sq m, or approximately 129.17 sq ft. Convert both dimensions to the same unit before multiplying; this calculator currently accepts feet or inches only.

Measuring an L-shaped room

Divide an L-shaped floor into two non-overlapping rectangles. Measure the length and width of each rectangle, calculate each area separately, and add the two results. Do not count the shared corner twice. For a wall or floor with permanent openings, subtract those areas only when the estimate calls for net usable surface.

Common area-measurement mistakes

  • Multiplying feet by inches without converting one of the units first.
  • Confusing a linear foot with a square foot: area requires two dimensions.
  • Using rounded measurements for a material order without checking the actual site dimensions.

Useful conversions: 1 square yard = 9 sq ft; 1 square metre is approximately 10.7639 sq ft. Record the original unit with the result so measurements can be checked later.

Area measurement guide and common mistakes

For a rectangular room, multiply length by width after converting both measurements to the same unit. A room measuring 12 ft by 10 ft has an area of 120 square feet. If measurements are in inches, divide the product of length and width by 144 to convert square inches to square feet. Measure wall-to-wall at floor level and note alcoves, columns and irregular corners separately.

For L-shaped or irregular rooms

Divide the floor plan into simple rectangles, calculate each area, then add them together. Avoid counting an overlapping section twice. For flooring purchases, add a separate allowance for cutting, breakage and pattern matching; the correct allowance depends on tile size, layout and installer recommendations.

Frequently asked questions

Is square feet the same as square foot?

They refer to the same area unit; “square foot” is singular and “square feet” is plural.

How do I convert square inches to square feet?

Divide square inches by 144, because one foot is 12 inches and 12 × 12 = 144 square inches.

Should I include wastage?

Measure the actual area first, then add a separate material allowance appropriate for the job. Wastage is not part of the room’s geometric area.

Can I use this for land area?

It can calculate rectangular areas, but land boundaries may be irregular. Use surveyed dimensions for legal or property transactions.

How to Calculate Square Feet and Convert Units

Square feet measure area, not length. For a rectangular room or plot, measure length and width and express both in feet before multiplying. If dimensions are in different units, convert them first; mixing feet and metres produces an incorrect result.

Formula and method

Area (ft²) = Length (ft) × Width (ft); 1 m² ≈ 10.7639 ft²

Worked example

A room measuring 12 ft by 10 ft has an area of 120 square feet. A 3 m by 4 m room has an area of 12 m², or approximately 129.17 ft². For L-shaped rooms, calculate each rectangle separately and add the areas.

Frequently asked questions

Is square feet the same as feet?

No. Feet measure length; square feet measure two-dimensional area.

How do I measure an irregular room?

Split it into simple rectangles or triangles, calculate each area and add the results.

Is 1 square metre exactly 10.76 square feet?

10.7639 is a useful conversion approximation; more decimal places may matter for precision work.

Can area alone tell me construction cost?

No. Rates, material specifications, labour, site conditions and measurement conventions also affect the final cost.
