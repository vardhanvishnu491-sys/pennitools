Area

How to calculate square feet

Measurements in inches: Length × Width ÷ 144 = square feet.

Measurements in feet: Length × Width = square feet.

For example, a 10 ft × 12 ft room has an area of 120 sq ft. If measurements are 77 inches × 33 inches, divide the product by 144 to convert the result to square feet.

Where a square feet calculator is useful

Use this calculator for room measurements, flooring estimates, ceiling areas, walls, furniture panels, plywood work, carpentry and basic construction planning. Keeping the measurement unit consistent helps avoid calculation errors.

Square feet vs square inches

One square foot contains 144 square inches. That is why measurements given in inches use the ÷ 144 formula. When both dimensions are already in feet, simply multiply length by width.
