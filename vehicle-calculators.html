Choosing the right vehicle calculator

Use a mileage calculator when you know distance and fuel consumed, a fuel-cost calculator to budget a journey, and an EV range or charging-cost calculator to estimate battery use and charging expense. A tyre-size comparison helps compare nominal dimensions, but it cannot confirm that a tyre will physically fit a particular vehicle or comply with manufacturer recommendations.

Keep estimates comparable

For fuel comparisons, use the same distance units and a realistic mileage figure. For electric vehicles, charging losses, temperature, speed, load, terrain and accessory use can change real-world range. For loan or trip estimates, check whether tolls, fees, insurance and other expenses are included before comparing totals.

Frequently asked questions

Are the results manufacturer-certified?

No. They are calculations based on the values you enter and are not official vehicle ratings.

Can a tyre calculator guarantee fitment?

No. Check the vehicle manual, load and speed ratings, rim compatibility, clearance and local regulations with a qualified tyre professional.

Why is actual fuel or EV range different?

Traffic, temperature, driving style, load, road conditions and vehicle condition all affect consumption.

Should I use these results to make a purchase decision alone?

Use them as one input, then verify specifications, costs and safety requirements from the relevant manufacturer or provider.
