How the EMI calculator works

This loan EMI calculator estimates your monthly payment from the loan amount, annual interest rate and repayment period. It also shows estimated total interest and total repayment.

EMI formula

For a standard reducing-balance loan, EMI depends on the principal, monthly interest rate and number of monthly payments. A longer tenure can reduce the monthly EMI but usually increases total interest paid.
