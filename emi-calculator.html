How the EMI calculator works

This loan EMI calculator estimates your monthly payment from the loan amount, annual interest rate and repayment period. It also shows estimated total interest and total repayment.

What affects your EMI

Your principal, monthly interest rate and number of monthly payments determine the EMI. A longer tenure can reduce the monthly EMI but usually increases total interest paid.

EMI formula

For a standard fixed-rate loan with monthly repayments, the reducing-balance EMI formula is:

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1)

P is the original principal, r is the monthly interest rate expressed as a decimal (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100), and n is the total number of monthly instalments. If the interest rate is zero, the estimated EMI is principal divided by the number of instalments.

Worked example: ₹5 lakh over five years

For a ₹5,00,000 loan at 10% annual interest over 60 months, the monthly rate is about 0.8333%. The estimated EMI is about ₹10,624, total repayment is about ₹6,37,411, and total interest is about ₹1,37,411. Your displayed result may differ by a rupee or two due to rounding.

How tenure changes the cost

A longer tenure generally lowers the monthly instalment but increases the number of months on which interest is paid. A shorter tenure generally raises the EMI while reducing total interest, assuming the same rate and repayment method. Compare both the monthly payment and total repayment rather than choosing a loan based on EMI alone.

Frequently asked questions

What is an EMI?

An equated monthly instalment is a scheduled monthly payment toward a loan. For a fixed-rate reducing-balance loan, each payment generally includes interest on the outstanding balance and repayment of principal.

What formula is used?

For principal P, monthly rate r and number of monthly payments n, EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1). The monthly rate is the annual percentage rate divided by 12 and then by 100. At zero interest, the payment is P ÷ n.

Why can the bank’s EMI differ?

Fees, insurance, disbursement timing, daily-interest conventions, rounding and variable rates can change the actual repayment schedule. This calculator uses the entered rate and term as a simplified estimate.

Should I choose the loan with the lowest EMI?

Not on that basis alone. A longer tenure can lower the monthly instalment while increasing total interest. Compare total repayment, fees, rate type and the effect of prepayment before deciding.
