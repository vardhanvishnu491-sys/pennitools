How to interpret an EV range estimate

The simple estimate multiplies usable battery energy in kWh by distance efficiency in km/kWh. For example, a 40 kWh usable battery and an assumed 6 km/kWh efficiency give 40 × 6 = 240 km. Use usable battery capacity rather than assuming all advertised gross capacity is available for driving.

Why real range changes

  • Highway speeds generally use more energy per kilometre than steady moderate speeds.
  • Air-conditioning or heating, hills, payload, tyre pressure, rain and temperature can change efficiency.
  • Keep a reserve for charging availability and unexpected detours rather than planning to arrive at 0%.
  • Vehicle displays and manufacturer range figures use different test conditions; this tool is a simple estimate, not a guarantee.

Use your own recent trip efficiency when possible. Check the vehicle manual and current charging network information before a long journey.

Understand your electric vehicle range estimate

Estimated range is a planning aid, not a promise of how far an EV will travel. A simple estimate divides usable battery energy by energy consumption. If a vehicle has 60 kWh usable and averages 15 kWh per 100 km, estimated range is about 400 km (60 ÷ 15 × 100). Actual usable capacity and consumption depend on the vehicle, battery condition and conditions on the road.

What changes real-world range?

Highway speed, cold or very hot weather, cabin heating or cooling, hills, payload, tyre pressure and driving style can all affect energy use. Plan a buffer and identify charging stops before a long trip, especially when chargers are sparse or weather is extreme. Do not plan to arrive at a charger with no reserve.

Frequently asked questions

Is the displayed range guaranteed?

No. It is a mathematical estimate from your inputs, not a manufacturer guarantee.

Should I use total or usable battery capacity?

Use usable capacity when known, because the full battery capacity may not be available for driving.

Why is highway range often lower?

Higher speeds increase aerodynamic drag and may increase energy consumption per kilometre.

How much reserve should I keep?

Choose a conservative buffer based on weather, route, charger reliability and your vehicle. This calculator cannot assess live charger availability.
