How to use the GST calculator

This GST calculator helps you add GST to a base amount or remove GST from a GST-inclusive price. Enter the amount, choose a GST rate and select the calculation mode to see the base amount, tax amount and final amount.

Adding GST

When adding GST, the calculator applies the selected rate to the base amount. For example, 18% GST on ₹1,000 is ₹180, giving a final price of ₹1,180.

Removing GST from an inclusive price

When the entered amount already includes GST, the calculator separates the taxable base from the GST portion. This is useful when checking invoices, receipts and quoted prices.

GST rates and tax rules can change. Verify the applicable rate for your goods or services with an official or qualified source before filing or making a tax decision.
