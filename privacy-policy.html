Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

PenniTools respects your privacy. This notice explains how information may be handled when you use the website.

Browser-based tools

Many calculators and some document, image and transcription features operate in your browser. When a tool is designed for local processing, the information you enter or files you select are not intentionally uploaded to a PenniTools server by that tool.

Third-party services

Some features may use third-party libraries, APIs, content delivery networks, model providers or other services. Those providers may receive technical information or process requests according to their own privacy policies.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

If analytics or advertising are enabled, third-party providers may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies to measure usage, prevent abuse, personalize or measure advertising, and maintain service functionality. The providers' current policies and applicable consent or privacy controls apply.

Information you enter

Do not enter passwords, bank credentials, identity numbers, confidential business information or other sensitive information into a tool unless it specifically requires it and you understand how it is processed.

Security

We aim to use HTTPS and reasonable technical safeguards. No website or browser-based system can guarantee absolute security.

Children

PenniTools is a general-purpose website and is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated as features and services change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Important: This is a general website privacy notice, not legal advice. Actual providers and configuration should be reviewed before enabling analytics, advertising or new data-processing features.
