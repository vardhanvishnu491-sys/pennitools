Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

PenniTools respects your privacy. This page explains the general way information may be handled when you use the website.

Browser-based tools

Many PenniTools calculators and document/image tools operate directly in your browser. When a tool is designed for local processing, the selected file or entered calculation data is not intentionally uploaded to a PenniTools server by that tool.

Third-party services

Some features may load libraries or services from third parties. For example, browser AI models may be downloaded from a content delivery or model provider, and currency conversion may request current exchange-rate data from a public third-party service. Those services may process technical information according to their own policies.

Cookies and advertising

PenniTools may use cookies or similar technologies if analytics, advertising or other services are enabled in the future. Advertising providers such as Google may use cookies to serve and measure advertisements in accordance with their policies and applicable choices or controls.

Information you enter

Do not enter passwords, bank credentials, identity numbers, confidential business information or other sensitive information into a tool unless the tool specifically requires it and you understand how it is processed.

Security

We aim to use HTTPS and reasonable technical safeguards. No website or browser-based system can guarantee absolute security.

Children

PenniTools is a general-purpose website and is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated as features and services change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Important: This is a general website privacy notice, not legal advice. Before enabling advertising or collecting analytics, the site's actual providers and configuration should be reviewed and this policy updated where necessary.
