How to use this speech-to-text tool

  1. Open this page in a browser that supports its speech-recognition feature.
  2. Choose the language you will speak, then allow microphone access when prompted.
  3. Select Start Transcription and speak clearly at a steady pace.
  4. Select Stop Transcription, review the text, correct names and punctuation, then copy it.

Troubleshooting

If the microphone does not start, check the browser's site permissions, confirm the correct microphone is connected, and make sure the page is using HTTPS. Some browsers do not support speech recognition or may send audio to a browser provider for processing. Availability and language quality depend on the browser and device.

Accuracy and privacy

This tool uses browser speech recognition; it is not an uploaded-audio transcription service and does not provide speaker diarization. Background noise, accents, overlapping speech and technical vocabulary can reduce accuracy. Review important transcripts before using them as records. Do not dictate confidential or sensitive information unless you are comfortable with the processing terms of your browser provider.

Speech-to-text: prepare audio for better results

Speech recognition works best when one person speaks clearly at a steady pace with limited background noise. A built-in browser speech recognition interface is different from uploading an existing recording to a transcription service; this page uses browser speech recognition and may require microphone permission and a supported browser. It does not guarantee a verbatim transcript.

Before you begin

  1. Use a quiet room and keep the microphone at a consistent distance.
  2. Allow microphone access only if you intend to dictate live speech.
  3. Speak in complete phrases and pause between topics or speakers.
  4. Review names, numbers, dates, technical terms and punctuation before copying the transcript.

Frequently asked questions

Can I upload an MP3 recording here?

This page uses browser speech recognition, not an uploaded-audio transcription workflow. Use a tool specifically designed for audio-file transcription if you need to process a recording.

Why is the microphone button unavailable?

Check browser support, microphone permission, system input settings and whether the page is served over a secure HTTPS connection.

Why are some words wrong?

Noise, accents, overlapping speakers, low volume and specialist vocabulary can affect recognition. Review the text against the original speech.

Is the transcript suitable as an official record?

Not without careful verification. Do not rely on automated recognition alone for legal, medical, financial or other high-stakes records.

Privacy reminder: Browser recognition may involve services provided by your browser vendor. Review the browser provider’s terms and avoid dictating sensitive information unless you understand the processing.
