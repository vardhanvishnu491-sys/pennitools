Free Image Tools

Process images directly in your browser. Keep the existing crop, resize, compress and JPG/PNG conversion tools, plus useful photo and format utilities.

🔒 Privacy-first: images are processed locally in your browser and are not uploaded to a PenniTools server.

📐 Resize Image

Resize an image to an exact pixel size.

🗜️ Compress Image

🔄 JPG ↔ PNG

Convert an image to JPG or PNG.

🖼️ WebP Converter

Convert JPG, PNG or WebP images to JPG or WebP.
