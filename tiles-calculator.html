How to calculate tiles for a room

First calculate the room area by multiplying its length by width. Convert the room and tile measurements to the same unit, then divide the room area by the area covered by one tile. The calculator rounds up to a whole tile because tiles cannot normally be purchased as fractions.

Allow for tile wastage

Cutting, breakage, edge pieces and installation patterns can create waste. A 10% allowance is a useful starting point for a straightforward project, while rooms with many corners, diagonal layouts or complex patterns may require a different allowance.

Example tile calculation

For a 20 ft × 15 ft room, the floor area is 300 sq ft. If each tile covers 2 ft × 2 ft, one tile covers 4 sq ft, so 75 tiles are needed before wastage. With 10% wastage, the recommended quantity is rounded up to 83 tiles.
